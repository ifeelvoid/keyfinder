KeyFinder: data credits and licences

KeyFinder's code is MIT licensed. Its key profiles and key-model weights were fitted by
the KeyFinder project. They derive from these human annotations, released under
Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 (CC BY-SA 4.0):

- GiantSteps+ v2 key annotations. https://zenodo.org/records/4153506
- GiantSteps-MTG / Beatport EDM Key dataset. Annotators: Eduard Mas Marín and Ángel Faraldo.
  https://zenodo.org/records/1101082

The derived weights are offered under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 terms:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

The key model's training data also includes 31 tracks from IFEELVOID's own catalog.
Their filename labels were trusted only where they agreed with an independent free
detector. No catalog audio is distributed. KeyFinder includes no Essentia or madmom code or models.
